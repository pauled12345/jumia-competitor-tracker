# Competitor Intelligence Platform — Business Plan

## Product Name: Jumia Price Scout

## The Problem
Jumia sellers (especially in electronics) fly blind:
- They don't know when competitors change prices
- They don't notice when competitors go out of stock
- They can't track price trends over time
- Manual checking takes hours per day

## The Solution
An automated tool that:
1. Monitors competitor prices 24/7
2. Alerts sellers when prices drop or stock changes
3. Shows price history and trends
4. Suggests optimal repricing

## Target Customer
- Jumia sellers in electronics (TVs, phones, appliances)
- 50-200 active listings
- Doing KSh 500K-5M/month in sales
- Currently tracking competitors manually or not at all

## Revenue Model
| Tier | Price | Features |
|------|-------|----------|
| Starter | KSh 3,000/mo | 20 products, daily scrape, email alerts |
| Pro | KSh 7,500/mo | 100 products, 6-hourly scrape, WhatsApp alerts, repricing suggestions |
| Enterprise | KSh 15,000/mo | Unlimited products, hourly scrape, API access, dedicated support |

## Go-to-Market
1. **Week 1-2**: Build reliable scraper, test on 50+ live Jumia pages
2. **Week 3-4**: Build polished dashboard, auth, billing (M-Pesa)
3. **Week 5**: Onboard 5 beta sellers (free for 30 days)
4. **Week 6**: Collect testimonials, fix issues, launch paid tier
5. **Week 7-8**: Pitch to Vitron and other large sellers with case studies

## Competitive Advantage
- Built specifically for Jumia Kenya (not generic scraper)
- Scrapling handles anti-bot bypass (Playwright-based)
- Local market knowledge (KSh, M-Pesa, WhatsApp alerts)
- Affordable vs. international tools (DataForSEOx, Prisync)

## Key Risks
- Jumia blocks scraping → Mitigation: proxies, rate limiting, stealth mode
- Sellers don't pay → Mitigation: free trial, clear ROI demonstration
- Competitors copy → Mitigation: build fast, lock in customers with data history
